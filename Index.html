My Story

When life took everything away, I discovered what could never be taken from me.

Some people are born into comfort. Others are born into storms.

Rejection and abandonment marked her childhood. Yet at just eight years old, an encounter with God became the anchor that would hold through every storm to come — the loss of her brother, her husband, and her very sense of self; a crime syndicate that stripped away all she had built; and, during COVID, a fire that consumed what remained.

The devastating question

Who am I, and why am I here?

Instead, those moments became the birthplace of a deeper purpose.

Her story is not about what she lost. It’s about what became possible when she chose not to surrender.

From the Ashes

Turning Pain into Purpose

The profound losses of her brother and husband set Dr. Madelaine on a quest that would change the course of her life forever.

Determined to understand life, death, healing, and human potential, she pursued advanced studies in Metaphysical Science, becoming a Doctor and Reverend while also training as a Pranic Healer. Along the way, she faced her own health challenges and a near-death experience that deepened her understanding of the connection between mind, body, spirit, and faith.

Yet her greatest lessons would come through further adversity.

The loss of her home, business, and financial security through criminal activity forced her to rebuild from the ground up. What emerged from that season became the foundation of the I AM POSSIBLE breakthrough programs — transformational teachings that have been empowering individuals and organizations internationally for nearly a decade.

Then, in 2022, another life-altering event occurred when a fire destroyed her remaining belongings. What initially appeared to be another devastating loss became an unexpected gift: freedom from the weight of the past, and permission to fully embrace her future.

Today, Dr. Madelaine works at the intersection of neuroscience, mindset renewal, trauma recovery, spiritual faith, and human transformation. Through keynote presentations, workshops, and coaching programs, she helps professionals, leaders, and individuals navigate life’s crossroads, overcome adversity, and discover new possibilities beyond their current circumstances.
